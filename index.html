Chapter 01

Where It All Began

Satori Residences | DMCI Homes

It all started at a construction site, where a Civil Engineer and a Mechanical Engineer first crossed paths.

After long and tiring days at work, we found ourselves in an open space where we could unwind, feel the gentle evening breeze, and gaze at the stars above. What began as a simple moment of rest soon turned into hours of storytelling, laughter, playful banter, and genuine conversations.

Little did we know that those quiet evenings would become the beginning of a love story neither of us had ever imagined.

Chapter 02

The Beginning of Us

February 1, 2021

The day finally came when we decided to take a chance on each other. What started as two engineers sharing stories after work slowly blossomed into something more meaningful.

Neither of us could have predicted how our story would unfold, but with every passing day, we discovered more reasons to choose each other. Looking back, we are forever grateful for the little moments that led us here and for the love we have continued to build together.

Chapter 03

Adventures Together

One of the things we love most about our relationship is traveling and discovering new places together. From the beautiful beaches of Cebu, Bohol, Bantayan Island, Siargao, Siquijor, Dumaguete, Boracay, Coron, and Caramoan to the mountains of Sagada and Baguio, and even beyond the Philippines to Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Vietnam, each destination has given us memories to treasure.

Through every journey, we have shared laughter, tried new things, and experienced moments that brought us closer. We have learned that it is not just about the places we visit, but the person beside us who makes every experience meaningful.

With so many places explored and countless more on our bucket list, we look forward to all the adventures still waiting for us. Because no matter where life takes us, our favorite part of every journey will always be having each other by our side.

Chapter 04

The Proposal

February 14, 2025 · A Magical Yes at Hong Kong Disneyland

Amid the enchanting atmosphere of Hong Kong Disneyland, one of the happiest places on earth, a beautiful new chapter of our love story began.

Surrounded by magic, wonder, and the joy of being together, one heartfelt question turned an already unforgettable day into a memory we will cherish for a lifetime. With a joyful “Yes,” we took one step closer to forever, ready to build a life filled with love, laughter, and countless adventures together.

And just like the happiest fairytales, our story was only beginning.

Chapter 05

Forever Begins

February 6, 2027

And now, our favorite chapter begins.

With hearts full of love and gratitude, we invite you to celebrate with us as we say “I do” and begin our forever together.

From unexpected conversations at a construction site to a lifetime of dreams waiting to unfold, every moment has led us to this beautiful day.

Our greatest adventure is not a destination, but a lifetime of choosing each other.

Here’s to forever, together.
